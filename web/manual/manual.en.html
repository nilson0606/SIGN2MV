1. Overview

SIGN2MV puts singing and recording, AI covers and lyric music videos in one program with one song library:

  • Library — import a song once (YouTube or a file). Its separated stems are computed once and shared by every workspace.
  • Sing & record — sing along with pitch guidance and scoring, record whole songs or segments, then edit and save the takes.
  • AI cover — convert the song's vocal with an RVC voice model, mix it, keep versions, fix sections, train your own voice models.
  • MV — a video straight from the audio (quick MV), or a 1080p 60 fps lyric video from photos and lyrics with Whisper lyric alignment and karaoke colouring. The audio can be the original, any of your recordings or any cover version.

Everything runs on this computer. Nothing is uploaded. The heavy work (separation, voice conversion, Whisper, video rendering, training) runs one job at a time on the graphics card; lighter jobs run beside it.

No songs and no voice models come with the program. You import your own songs; a voice model is one you train yourself or import because you have the right to use it (chapter 8).

2. Getting started, step by step

The first time, follow these steps in order. Each step says what to do and what you see when it worked; go on only when you see it.

Step 1: Check the computer

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). macOS and Linux are not supported yet.
  • Internet: the first installation downloads about 10 GB. Importing from YouTube needs it too.
  • Disk: besides those 10 GB, your songs, stems, recordings and videos are kept in the same folder, so leave room.
  • An NVIDIA graphics card is recommended. Without one everything works, but separation, covers and training are much slower.
  • For singing: a microphone and headphones.

Step 2: Download

  1. Open https://github.com/nilson0606/SIGN2MV in a browser.
  2. Press the green Code button near the top right, then Download ZIP at the bottom of its menu.

It worked when: SIGN2MV-main.zip is in the browser's downloads.

Step 3: Extract it to a folder with an English path

  1. In File Explorer, right-click SIGN2MV-main.zip → Properties. If there is an Unblock box at the bottom, tick it and press OK. (No box: skip this.)
  2. Right-click it again → Extract All…, choose D:\ (or another drive) and press Extract.
  3. Rename the extracted folder SIGN2MV-main to SIGN2MV.

It worked when: D:\SIGN2MV directly contains install.bat, start.bat and MANUAL.html (double-click it to open this manual; nothing needs to be installed). If you see another SIGN2MV-main folder inside, it was wrapped once more: move its contents up.

Use only English letters and digits in the folder's full path (D:\SIGN2MV is fine; a path with Chinese characters is not). With such a path the voice model index cannot be read.

Step 4: Install

  1. In D:\SIGN2MV, double-click install.bat. A black window opens and starts downloading and installing.
  2. If Windows shows a blue Windows protected your PC box, press More info, then Run anyway.
  3. At the very start (before anything is downloaded) it asks Download the Sucial model? [y/N]: an optional model for male/female duets, for non-commercial use only. If you accept that, type y and press Enter; otherwise just press Enter (duets then use another model and still work).
  4. Wait until it finishes; the first time takes a while, depending on the connection. If it stops, double-click install.bat again: finished steps are skipped.

It worked when: the black window ends with Done. Double-click start.bat to open SIGN2MV. Press any key to close it.

Step 5: Start the program

  1. Double-click start.bat. A black console window (the server) opens, then the SIGN2MV window.
  2. Keep the black window open while you work (it can be minimised).

It worked when: the SIGN2MV window shows Home; under System status, Installation says Complete and Compute device names your graphics card (or the CPU without an NVIDIA card). The interface language follows Windows (English unless Windows is in Traditional Chinese) and can be changed at the top.

Step 6: Import your first song

  1. Press Library at the top.
  2. Type the song's title in the box at the top left and press Add song.
  3. Under Import music on the right (the new song is selected), paste a YouTube link and press Import from YouTube (or drop an audio or video file from your computer on the dashed box).

It worked when: it says Import finished and the song shows a folded ▸ Original section (open it for the original player) and three buttons: Sing this song, Make an MV, AI cover.

Step 7: Sing once and get a score

  1. Press Sing this song: the Sing & Record page opens with the song.
  2. The first time, the page asks you to press Prepare song reference. Press it and wait (it separates the vocal and extracts its pitch; a few minutes the first time) until the status says ready.
  3. Put on headphones. Press Enable microphone and allow the microphone when the browser asks. Speak: the Input level should move.
  4. Press Sing from the start and score; the video starts from the beginning. Sing along.
  5. At the end (or whenever you want to stop) press Finish and score.

It worked when: the total score and the pitch, timing and completeness scores appear; the take waits under Whole-song recordings · Pending review, where you can listen to it and press Save for post-production if you like it.

A low score, or a pitch line that is always a little behind the original, usually means the input latency is off (400 ms by default; it differs per computer). Measure it with the latency test on the sing page, or try a few values with Rescore (chapter 6) without singing again.

Step 8: Get a voice model

An AI cover needs a voice model, and none comes with the program. Press AI Cover at the top and, under Voice models on the right, either:

  • Train your own voice: collect clean solo singing or speech, about 10 minutes or more in total, add the files under Train a voice model and press 🚀 Start training. Training runs on the graphics card for a while.
  • Import a model: an RVC model you have the right to use (.pth, usually with an .index), with Import from a folder or Upload a model.

It worked when: the model's name is in the model list and can be chosen under Voice model on the left.

Step 9: Make your first cover

  1. On the AI cover page, choose the song at the top.
  2. On the left, choose your Voice model and set Pitch if needed (about −12 for a female song sung by a male voice, +12 the other way round, usually 0 for the same gender).
  3. Press 🎤 Make cover (new version). The first time it separates the song first; the progress shows under the button.

It worked when: version #1 appears under Versions on the right and plays.

Step 10: Make your first video

  1. On version #1 press Quick MV (the Quick MV tab of the MV page opens with that audio chosen).
  2. Check the title and singer and press Make quick MV.

It worked when: the video is in the list below and plays; it can be downloaded as MP4. For a lyric video with photos, see chapter 9.

Step 11: Shut down

Press Shut down at the top right. It worked when: the SIGN2MV window says it has shut down and the black console window closes by itself within a few seconds. Next time, start at step 5.

3. Installation details

The steps are in chapter 2. What the installer does:

  • Nothing needs to be installed first, not even Python. install.bat downloads the uv Python manager (checked against its checksum), Python 3.12, the packages of each environment, ffmpeg, Applio (voice conversion) and the AI models.
  • Everything goes inside the SIGN2MV folder (runtime\, models\), caches and temporary files included, not on C:. The change in free space on C: during installation is shown on the Home page.
  • If installation stops (network drop, full disk), run install.bat again: finished steps are skipped. The log is in logs\install.log. On the Home page, Installation and the checks list show what is ready.
  • An NVIDIA graphics card is used automatically when present. Without one everything still works on the CPU, only slower (training on the CPU is very slow).
  • MV encoding tries the NVIDIA (NVENC, driver 570 or newer), AMD and Intel graphics card encoders in turn and uses the CPU only if none works; the installer writes which one works to logs\install.log.
  • FFmpeg is a fixed version checked against its checksum, so every PC gets the same file.
  • The last step compiles every environment's Python files once, so separation, covers and Whisper start faster.

Updating

To update, copy the new files over the old folder; your songs, recordings, voice models and settings are not touched (library, models, runtime and config are not in the ZIP).

  1. Press Shut down at the top right of SIGN2MV and wait until the window closes.
  2. On the GitHub page, Code → Download ZIP; right-click the ZIP → Properties → tick Unblock → OK (skip if there is no such box).
  3. Extract it anywhere; you get a SIGN2MV-main folder.
  4. Open SIGN2MV-main, select everything (Ctrl + A), copy, and paste it into your SIGN2MV folder (the one with start.bat); when asked, choose Replace the files in the destination.
  5. Double-click start.bat. Code date under System status on the home page ends with a 7-character code (e.g. 2026-11-08 · 0f0d724). When it matches the 7-character code at the top right of the file list on GitHub, you are up to date. Compare the code, not just the date: there can be several changes on one day.

To find out whether there is a new version, you can also press Check for updates under System status on the home page: it says whether you are up to date and how many newer changes GitHub has (it only asks; nothing is downloaded or replaced).

Run install.bat again only when CHANGELOG.md says so; running it when unsure does no harm, finished parts are skipped.

4. Start and shut down

  1. Double-click start.bat. A console window (the server) and a SIGN2MV window open (an app window of Microsoft Edge, which comes with Windows, at http://127.0.0.1:9700). Keep the console window open while you work.
  2. To finish, press Shut down at the top right. If jobs are running or a workspace is busy (recording, converting, rendering), SIGN2MV lists them and asks before cancelling them. The console window then closes by itself within a few seconds.

The interface language follows Windows: Chinese on Windows in Traditional Chinese (Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macau), English on Windows in any other language, Simplified Chinese included; change it with the language menu at the top. Manual ↗ at the top opens this page. The program only accepts connections from this computer; other computers on the network cannot reach it.

5. Song library

  1. Open Library, type a title in the box at the top left and press Add song.
  2. Import the music: paste a YouTube URL (or press 📋 to paste it) and press Import from YouTube (the video is downloaded too, up to 1080p). As soon as a link is pasted a preview card shows the thumbnail, title, length and video ID, with Open the original page and ▶ Preview to watch it before importing. Or choose an audio/video file and press Upload a file, or drop the file on the dashed box (it uploads at once, with a progress bar; up to 2 GB and 15 minutes). The original is kept unchanged.
  3. When the import finishes, the song shows a folded ▸ Original section (open it for the original player) and three buttons: Sing this song, Make an MV, AI cover.

Under the video in Original, Download MP3 saves the song’s original audio as a 192 kbps MP3 (made from the lossless source audio, so it is compressed only once; a few seconds for a 4-minute song). Download in the video’s ⋮ menu is the browser’s own and saves the MP4 video.

Stems and key

Under Stems and key you can prepare and listen to the separated tracks: choose the separator (Demucs, BS-RoFormer, Mel-RoFormer Kim), optionally a second pass on the instrumental and a lead/backing split, and a key. Separation is cached per song: any workspace that needs the same stems reuses them.

Saved stems (below the Prepare button) lists every separation and key copy this song keeps, as a tree: a lead/backing split sits under the separation it was made from. Each row shows its tracks, size, date and which cover versions or sing & record references use it. Delete removes that copy and everything made from it. Nothing breaks: whatever uses it separates again the next time it needs it (that takes a few minutes). Deleting waits while a job for the song is running.

Replacing the source: a song that already has its original shows a folded ▸ Import again (replace source) at the top; open it to replace the original with a new link or file. The old original is removed only once the new one is fully in place; if that fails or the program is stopped in between, the old one is put back. After a new source, older sing references are marked Source replaced: made again when loaded and are made again when loaded.

Click a section title (Original, Stems and key, Saved stems, Storage) to fold or unfold it; the page remembers which ones you keep open. A folded section still shows a one-line summary. In Saved stems, ▸ N below shows or hides the copies made from that row.

Storage

The library list shows each song's size, and under it the library total and the free space on the drive. A song's Storage section splits its size into original, stems & key copies, finished AI covers, cover cache, sing & record, MV projects and other, with where to clean up each one:

  • Stems & key copies: delete them one by one in Saved stems.
  • AI covers and their cache: delete versions on the AI cover page; the cache that only those versions used is cleaned up with them. 🧹 Clean up this song's unused files (above the versions) lists left-over converted vocals, section pieces and key previews with their sizes and deletes them after you confirm.
  • Sing & record: delete takes and prepared references on the Sing & record page.
  • MV: delete projects on the MV page.

Rename changes the title. Delete removes the song and everything made from it (stems, recordings, covers, MVs) after a confirmation.

Finished folder

The song folders in the library are named by id, which is hard to find your way around in Explorer. SIGN2MV keeps a Finished folder in the library (the same English name in every UI language): one folder per song, named by its title, with shortcuts you can double-click. Nothing is copied, so it takes no extra space.

  • Cover versions: Cover #1 MyVoice -12 · Key -2
  • Saved sing & record versions: Sing 10-31 09.05
  • Quick MVs: Quick MV Studio 1080p · Cover #1 · 10-31 09.20
  • Full MVs (once rendered): MV project title

A pitch or Key of 0 is left out of a cover name, e.g. Cover #2 MyVoice. Times use a dot (09.05) because Windows file names cannot hold ":". When two songs have the same title, their folders get the import date, e.g. Song (imported 10-29), and the time as well when they were imported on the same day. Names that still clash within a song get (2), (3).

Renaming a song renames its folder. Deleting a version, an MV or the song deletes its shortcuts. Only the shortcuts SIGN2MV made are ever deleted; files you put in the folders are left alone (a folder that still holds your files stays). A shortcut you delete by hand comes back at the next change; delete finished work you no longer want in SIGN2MV.

📂 Finished folder on the library page opens the whole folder; 📂 Finished in a song's details opens that song's folder (Windows).

6. Sing & record

Songs come from the library: choose one under Song (SIGN2MV library) at the top of the Sing & Record page and press Load song (or press Sing this song in the library). Keys that already have a singing reference are listed separately (e.g. “Song · Down 4 semitones”); those load right away, otherwise the page selects the key and asks you to press Prepare song reference. Help ↗ at the top of the page explains every control in detail.

Prepare the song

  • Key: below the player, the original key or −12 to +12 semitones (tempo unchanged). With a song loaded, press Create / Load this key after choosing.
    Keep the singer's timbre in the vocals, under the key (off by default): the original vocals keep the singer's own timbre (formants) when the key changes, so it sounds more like the singer singing in that key, but it may add some artefacts; the accompaniment is shifted as usual. On and off are two separate key versions, each saved, so you can switch between them to compare. Versions made with the old method on 2026-11-07 are listed as singer's timbre kept (old method): they still load and their recordings can still be post-processed, but they cannot be made again; make a new version for the new method's sound.
  • Press Prepare song reference and wait until the status says ready. The default is the full song (up to 15 minutes), Demucs separation and RMVPE pitch. Separation and pitch settings can change the analysis range (try only the first 15, 30 or 60 seconds, separated on their own without touching the whole-song stems), the separator and a lead/backing split.
  • Untick Keep separated stems to keep only the pitch reference: the stems this preparation made are removed afterwards (previews and backing-only playback then need them rebuilt).

Input and latency

  • Input mode: Browser capture by default. If enabling the microphone makes the backing audio quieter, choose Native capture (Python on this computer records the microphone; the first time Windows asks to allow Python to use it).
  • Press Enable microphone and check that the Input level moves. Wear headphones so the original vocal does not leak from the speakers into the microphone.
  • Input latency (under Watch the beat, adjust timing): 400 ms by default, meaning your voice arrives 400 ms after the song. It differs per computer and microphone; measure it with the Do Re Mi Fa Sol latency test and press Apply suggested compensation.

Sing the whole song and get a score

  1. Scoring settings: Octave matching (octave differences are allowed by default, for voices in another range than the original), Scoring range (the performed range only by default) and Scoring difficulty (standard, strict or relaxed).
  2. Choose what you hear, Original vocals + backing (default) or Backing / harmonies only. A version with separate lead and harmony vocals shows the default as Original vocals + harmonies + backing and adds Original vocals + backing (no harmonies) (only the lead and the accompaniment). The two non-default choices mute the video and play the separated stems. Then choose what is recorded: Singer, Singer + backing / harmonies (default) or Do not save recordings.
  3. Headphone mono (off by default, stereo): the guide plays the same sound in both ears, for singing with one earcup off so your own voice does not sound muffled; it also applies to segment recording. Keep the free earcup away from the microphone so the backing is not recorded. The choice is remembered.
  4. Headphone sound: Left ear / Right ear (both on by default): untick one to mute that ear. Singing with one earcup off, mute the free side and nothing leaks into the microphone; it also lets you compare singing with the left or the right ear. Both ears are on again each time the page opens. With Headphone mono on, the ear you wear hears both channels. Sent next to them shows what the page actually sends to each ear.
    While Windows Mono audio (Settings → Accessibility → Audio) is on, Windows mixes left and right after every program, so the ear switches and Headphone mono have no effect; a yellow note under the switches says so. To use them, press Win+A → Accessibility → turn off Mono audio, and turn it back on after singing. When you press Shut down, SIGN2MV reminds you if it differs from when SIGN2MV started.
  5. Press Sing from the start and score. Press Finish and score at the end (it also finishes when the video ends).
  6. The total score is pitch 60%, timing 25% and completeness 15%. The take waits under Whole-song recordings · Pending review: listen, download or delete it, and press Save for post-production if you like it.

Rescore without singing again

Next to Finish and score are Take to rescore, Rescore and Scoring delay:

  • The take can be the one just sung, a saved recording of this song and key (whole-song takes and segment mixes), or an AI cover version of this song and key.
  • Change the scoring delay and press Rescore to see the same performance scored at another delay, to find the delay that suits your computer. Every rescore is added to the score history.
  • After a change, the next takes of this song use the new delay for now; another song or a reload goes back to the Input latency setting.
  • A saved recording is analysed with RMVPE for a few seconds the first time; the result is kept with the recording. For an AI cover version the delay starts at 0 and the version's own pitch shift (for example −12) is taken out, so the score says how well the conversion kept the melody.
  • Octave matching is saved with each recording; for older segment mixes saved without it, the current Octave matching setting is used.

Score the original vocal

🧪 Score the original vocal uses the separated original vocal instead of the microphone and runs exactly the same scoring and recording as your own take. It shows what the song's reference and scoring give the original singer, so a low score can be traced to the reference or to the singing. The original vocal skips the “hear it from the speakers, then sing” delay a singer has, so Delay the original vocal (200 ms by default) brings it in that much later. The take is labelled “original vocal test” and does not change your input latency.

Segment recording

  1. Press the bright Enable recording mode button. While the song plays, press Split here where you want a boundary (splits can be undone, merged and fine-tuned).
  2. Choose a segment and press Record this segment: the guide starts a little before the segment and the take is saved automatically after the tail (or press Stop / Save segment). Record each segment as often as you like.
  3. Once a segment is saved, the microphone is closed; Record this segment opens it again. While a take is being saved (“Saving this part's take…”) the next one cannot start.
  4. Choose one take per segment and press Assemble full mix. Mix content is Vocals + backing / harmonies (default) or Vocals only; a lead / harmony version also offers Vocals + backing (no harmonies), which leaves the harmonies out of the result. Tick Allow silence in unrecorded regions for parts not recorded yet, or use Add original vocal region to let the original singer take some parts.
  5. When you like it, press Save for post-production or download the WAV. A segment mix can be rescored too.

Recording post-production

Levels, vocal timing correction, softening, EQ, compression, reverb/echo, noise, sibilance and breath reduction, pitch correction, trimming and fades; A/B comparison on the same phrase; render again (the original vocal is always kept). Rescore recording scores a saved recording again with another delay or difficulty. A finished recording can be turned into an MV with Create music video: it is saved as MV audio of the song and the MV workspace opens with it.

Other

Unscored region masks (in Separation and pitch settings) keep misdetected passages, such as an instrument taken for a voice, out of the score.

Deleting a prepared song in the list also deletes the stems only it used and tells you how much was freed.

7. AI cover

Open AI cover (or press AI cover on a song in the library) and pick the song at the top. The left side is the form; the right side holds the versions, voice models and training.

Make a cover

  1. Voice model: choose a model (and, if it has them, one of its kept training epochs). The last model you used is selected for new songs. If the list is empty, import or train a model first (chapter 8).
  2. Pitch: semitones for the voice (about −12 for female→male, +12 for male→female). The buttons 0, −12 and +12 set it in one click.
  3. Key (whole song): −8 to +8. The accompaniment and original backing vocals are shifted with Rubber Band (tempo unchanged) and the voice conversion gets the same shift (converted backing vocals get it in their conversion as well). ▶ Preview this key plays the original in that key; the original button continues from the same second, so you can switch back and forth. Reset returns to the original key. If Rubber Band is missing the page says so and the key cannot be changed.
  4. Optional Conversion fine-tuning: consonant/breath protect (0.33), index rate (0.75), volume envelope (0 = follow the original singer's loudness), pitch detection (rmvpe), auto-tune, noise clean-up.
  5. Optional Separation and backing vocals: separator; vocals = convert the lead and keep the original backing vocals (default), convert all vocals, or split twice for songs with three or more voices; the lead split model (aufr33 / viperx, or becruily if you have downloaded it: the page shows where to put the 1.7 GB file and its download link); accompaniment single or second pass. The section says which separators are ready. When the lead is split off, Mix the backing vocals into the cover in the mix block decides whether they go back into the cover; changing it only needs a quick remix. Below it, Convert the backing vocals to my voice too (off by default): in many songs the lead singer sang the backing vocals; with it on they are converted with the same model and pitch (about as long again as the lead conversion; quick remix reuses it). Backing vocals with several voices on different notes at once may sound rough; listen and compare. The version name gets “Backing VC”.
  6. Mix: converted vocal volume (95%) and accompaniment (85%), with presets Vocal forward, Standard balance and Backing forward. Each fine-tuning group has Restore defaults.
  7. Check the This run uses line above the button (model, pitch + key, accompaniment key, duet sides). Tick Separate again this time to ignore the cached stems once.
  8. Press 🎤 Make cover (new version). The progress appears under the button and in the job queue; Log opens the running log and Cancel stops it. The finished box keeps its own Log button, so you can look back at how long each step took. When the window is in the background the tab title tells you when it is finished.

Separation and conversions are cached. Running again with the same settings reuses them; changing only the mix is fast. Each song keeps its own settings; a song covered for the first time starts from the defaults, only the voice model of the last cover is kept.

Versions

Every run creates a version (#1, #2, …) with its own MP3. On a version:

Load settingsputs that version's settings back into the form.
🎛️ Quick remixapplies the form's mix, vocal sections, volume sections, backing-vocal choice and duet volumes to this version and replaces its audio. Model, pitch, key and separation stay as they were.
Remix as new versionthe same, but keeps the old version and adds a new one.
Quick MVa video straight from this version (title and moving spectrum): opens the Quick MV tab of the MV page.
🎬 Make MVopens the full MV workspace with this version as the audio.
Download / Stemsdownload the MP3; listen to the original, separated tracks and the converted vocal used by this version.
Rename / Deletedeleting also deletes the version's audio; cached files no other version uses are cleaned up. If an MV project uses the version as its music, the confirmation names it (exported MV videos are kept).
📂shows the MP3 in Windows Explorer.
🎬 Watch quick MV / full MVappears when an MV was made from this version; plays it right there.

Tick versions to delete several at once (Select all, Delete selected). Each version shows its file format and size. While a version plays, the bars above the list show its spectrum. 📚 Every song's cover versions lists the versions of all songs with a search box; Open this song switches to it.

Every version also appears as MV audio of the song.

Vocal sections

Play a version or the original and use ⏱ to take the player time for From / to (times can be typed as 1:23.4 or 83.4). Each listed section has ▶ to listen from just before it, and can be edited or deleted; the fold title shows how many sections there are. For each section choose:

  • Original singer — this part uses the original vocal (level-matched), for example where someone else sings.
  • Mute — no vocal in this part.
  • Re-convert — this part is converted again with another model, pitch or settings, including auto-tune and noise clean-up (1 s of context on each side). Optionally the section is separated again with its own separator, lead split and lead split model; its accompaniment is then replaced as well.
  • External clip — upload your own recording or another version. It replaces the converted vocal only, or the whole mix if the clip contains accompaniment. The clip's 0 s is placed at From + offset; without an end time the section lasts as long as the clip. A vocal clip can also be converted with a voice model.

Volume sections

Change the volume of the converted vocal, the accompaniment or the backing vocals between two times (0–200%, 80 ms crossfades). ▶ listens to a section, ✏️ brings it back into the fields to change it.

Male / female duet

Turn on Use male / female duet: the vocals are split into a male and a female line (aufr33 model, or Sucial if installed). Each side has its own model, pitch, volume and fine-tuning, or keeps the original singer. Changing only the two volumes later is a quick remix. The fold title shows what each side uses; the page warns when both sides keep the original singer.

8. Voice models and training

No voice model comes with the program. Import an RVC model you have the right to use, or train one with your own voice.

Import models

  1. In AI cover → Voice models → Import from a folder, type a folder of model folders, for example D:\voice-models, and press Import (copy). Every model folder with a .pth is copied with its .index, notes and kept epochs. The source is not changed.
  2. A single model folder or a single .pth file works too, and Upload a model accepts a .pth and an optional .index.

Model names use English letters, digits and _ . -. A folder whose name has other characters is skipped; import it on its own and type a name.

The convergence panel shows a model's training: mel loss per epoch with a 5-epoch average, grid values and the value under the mouse, and a verdict (still improving / levelled off / rising again, compare an earlier version). The list below it has the kept epoch versions with their size: Use this version selects it in the model list, 🗑 deletes it (the final version stays). While a model trains, the curve updates live.

Train a voice model

  1. Type a model name (default My_Voice_Ver1), add recordings (upload files, or type folders/files on this computer, one per line), or press ⚡ Use this song's separated vocals. Clean solo singing or speech, about 10 minutes or more in total, works best. Use only your own voice, or a voice whose owner agreed.
  2. Defaults: 100 epochs (buttons 30 / 100 / 200), batch 8, a version saved every 10 epochs, 40 kHz.
  3. Feature model: contentvec by default (general). The other choice is chinese-hubert-base, a feature model trained on Chinese speech (downloaded the first time it is used, about 361 MB). The model remembers which one it was trained with and conversion follows it, so a different feature model needs a new training. Models that do not use contentvec are labelled in the list and menus, e.g. Chinese features.
  4. Press 🚀 Start training. The six steps run automatically: import, slicing, feature extraction (RMVPE + the chosen feature model), training, index, installing. The dashboard shows the step, a status light and cards for epoch, step, best loss and progress; Log shows the training output.
  5. When it finishes, the model is in the list with its epoch versions (one every Save every epochs, up to the last four kept; the last epoch is the final version) and loss curve. The training scratch folder is deleted.

⏹ Stop training stops it; the model is not installed. 🧹 Clean training leftovers removes scratch files of stopped trainings and shows the free space left on each drive. Training uses the graphics card, so other GPU jobs wait until it ends. A newly trained or imported model is selected in the form.

9. MV

The MV page has two tabs.

Quick MV

A video made straight from the audio, no lyrics, no photos. Two looks:

  • Card (default) — dark card, “COVER SESSION”, the title, “Cover｜singer” and frequency bars that move with the music (30 fps).
  • Studio — gradient background, title, subtitle, credits line, a badge at the top right, a record in the middle and spectrum bars along the bottom (60 fps). Subtitle, badge and credits are filled in from the chosen audio (voice model, pitch, key) and can be changed; leave subtitle or credits empty to leave that line out.

Both come in 1080p or 720p (faster, smaller file). The page remembers the look and size.

Lyric subtitles (studio look, optional): paste the lyrics, one line each, or an LRC with [mm:ss.xx] times, and press 🎙️ Time the lines. LRC times are used as they are; plain lines are matched to the singing by Whisper, which listens to the song's separated vocals once (a cover version or a take keeps the song's timing, so its times start from the original's). Spread evenly places the lines without Whisper. Then tune each line: ▶ plays it, −0.5 / +0.5 move it, 📍 sets its start to the player time, the times and text can be typed, 🗑 deletes it and ➕ Add a line at the player time adds one. The lines are saved per audio and burnt into the video as white text with an outline above the spectrum.

  1. Press Quick MV on a cover version or on a sing & record take, or open the Quick MV tab and choose the song and the audio (original, a cover version or a take).
  2. Check the title and the singer (filled in from Settings → Cover singer name) and press Make quick MV. A 4-minute song takes about a minute with an NVIDIA graphics card.
  3. The list below plays, downloads (MP4), shows in Explorer (📂) or deletes the song's quick MVs. All songs lists every song's quick MVs with a search box, select all, delete selected and delete all. They are stored with the song and counted under MV in its storage overview.

Full MV

A lyric video made from photos and lyrics. A project belongs to a song; open it from the library (Make an MV), from a recording or from a cover version.

  1. Create a project: choose the song, a project name, the title and credits. The cover singer and studio names on the title cards come from Settings.
  2. Audio: the song's original, a sing & record take, a cover version, or an uploaded file (any format; one the browser cannot play is converted to FLAC). If the chosen audio was deleted later (for example its cover version), the page says so.
  3. Photos: upload one photo per scene (batch upload fills the empty slots in order), up to 99. To change the order, drag a photo onto another card: it moves there and the photos in between shift by one (◀ / ▶ swap with the neighbour). ＋ Insert photos on a card asks for photos (choose several at once, or drag them onto the button) and adds one card per photo in front of that card; ＋ Blank adds one empty card. The lyrics and timing stay with their positions.
  4. Lyrics: paste the lyrics (or press 📋 Paste clipboard) with [Scene 01] markers and press the Whisper alignment button. Whisper listens to the separated vocals (from the song's stem cache) and times every line; you can adjust lines, interludes and karaoke timing afterwards.
  5. Preview single frames, then render: a 1080p 60 fps MP4 with karaoke colouring. Rendering is a GPU job; the previous MV is kept until the new one is finished. Verification frames are checked automatically.

📂 Open folder shows the finished MV in Explorer. 📤 Import project reads an exported project JSON: photos, the Whisper result, the finished MV and audio outside the library found at the paths in the JSON are copied into the project. If the song already has a project with that name you are asked whether to overwrite it or keep both.

The MV never shows the word “AI”: credits use the cover singer name from Settings.

10. Job queue

Jobs lists every job with its progress and elapsed time. GPU jobs (separation, conversion, Whisper, training) run one at a time; the others wait with their position. Full MV renders have their own lane, so a long render does not hold up a cover. General jobs (imports, key changes, quick remixes, quick MVs) run beside them. Cancel stops a job and its helper programs. Log shows a job's output (coloured, follows the end, copy and clear), also while it runs. Cover progress and logs name what is being converted, e.g. “1/2 Lead”, “2/2 Backing”, “✔ Converted Backing (35 s)”. Separation notices that look like warnings but are harmless (such as ONNX Runtime having no CUDA; our separation models use CUDA through PyTorch) are left out.

11. Settings

  • Library folder — where songs are stored (default library inside the SIGN2MV folder). Choose folder… opens a folder dialog. The folder must be a full path, not a drive root or a program folder, and writable; it cannot be changed while work is running.
  • Cover singer name and Studio name — shown on the MV title cards.
  • 🎛️ Look (top bar) — switches the colours: console → classic (green) → day (light). Only the look changes; the sing & record and full MV pages keep their own look. The choice is remembered.

12. Where things are stored

Everything a song uses is one of three kinds:

  • Original (source): kept as long as the song exists.
  • Caches: stems (stems, one copy per separation setting), key copies (keys, one copy per track and key), sing & record preparation, the cover cache. Every workspace shares them: whichever needs a copy first makes it, the others reuse it. Deleting a cache never breaks anything; it is made again when needed.
  • Your work: sing & record takes, AI cover versions, MV projects and videos. Deleting them is final. Takes and cover versions can be used as MV audio.

The cover's Pitch only changes the converted voice and is kept in the cover cache; the Key setting (in the library, sing & record and AI cover) uses the shared key copies.

Folder (inside SIGN2MV)Contents
library\songs\<song>\source (original), stems, keys, sing, renders (takes and cover versions used as MV audio), cover (cover versions and caches), mv (MV projects)
library\Finished\shortcuts to each song's finished work (see "Finished folder"); only shortcuts, deleting them loses nothing
library\recordings\sing & record takes of all songs (one folder per take: vocal, mix, settings)
models\AI models; models\voices = your RVC voice models
runtime\Python environments, Applio, ffmpeg, caches, temporary files, training scratch
config\, logs\settings, logs

To uninstall, shut SIGN2MV down and delete its folder (copy library and models\voices first if you want to keep them).

13. Troubleshooting and reporting problems

Windows protected your PC when opening install.bat or start.batPress More info → Run anyway. Next time, tick Unblock in the ZIP's properties before extracting (chapter 2, step 3).
“Cannot reach the server”The console window was closed. Run start.bat again.
The page says to reload or restartPress F5, or Shut down and run start.bat again.
A model / component is missingRun install.bat again; the Home page lists the checks.
Voice conversion is not installedThe Applio part of the installation is incomplete: run install.bat again.
Key change failsffmpeg without Rubber Band: run install.bat again.
A job is slowCheck in Jobs whether another GPU job (for example a training) is running.
MVs are slow and encoded on the CPUThe MV status line or the quick MV list gives the graphics card encoder's reason (also kept in encoder_errors.log next to the MV). NVIDIA needs driver 570 or newer; after a driver update nothing needs reinstalling.
Sing & record scores stay lowCheck that Octave matching allows octave differences (when your range differs from the original), then try a few scoring delays with Rescore. 🧪 Score the original vocal shows whether the song's reference is sound.
“Native capture interrupted” or “Segment input interrupted” while recordingInput has stopped; what was recorded is kept. Enable the input again (in segment mode just press Record this segment again). If it happens often, please report it (see below); capture records are in logs\native-capture.jsonl.
The black window stays after Shut downClose it with its X button; nothing is lost (the jobs have already stopped).
The voice index seems ignoredKeep the SIGN2MV folder path in English letters (for example D:\SIGN2MV): the index library cannot open paths with Chinese characters.

Server details are in logs\server.log, installation details in logs\install.log.

Reporting a problem

Open a new issue on GitHub Issues with:

  • what you did, what you expected and what happened (copy the error message or add a screenshot);
  • when it happened, the Windows version and the graphics card;
  • the relevant part of the logs: logs\server.log, logs\install.log for installation problems, logs\native-capture.jsonl for input problems. Look through it first: leave out any file names or paths you do not want to make public.
