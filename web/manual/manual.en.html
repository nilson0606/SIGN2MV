SIGN2MV

Sing & record, AI cover and lyric music videos in one program. This is the master (English) manual; the Chinese manual is a translation of it.

1. Overview

SIGN2MV replaces three older programs (the sing & record app, the AI cover studio and the MV studio) with one program and one song library:

  • Library — import a song once (YouTube or a file). Its separated stems are computed once and shared by every workspace.
  • Sing & record — sing along with pitch guidance and scoring, record whole songs or segments, mix and save takes.
  • AI cover — convert the song's vocal with an RVC voice model, mix it, keep versions, fix sections, train your own voice models.
  • MV — build a 1080p 60 fps lyric video from photos and lyrics, with Whisper lyric alignment and karaoke colouring. The audio can be the original, any of your recordings or any cover version.

Everything runs on this computer. Nothing is uploaded. The heavy work (separation, voice conversion, Whisper, video rendering, training) runs one job at a time on the graphics card; lighter jobs run beside it.

SIGN2MV does not read, change or stop the old programs. It uses its own folder and port 9700, so the old programs can stay installed.

2. Install

  1. Put the SIGN2MV folder where it should live (for example D:\AI_SIGN2MV). Everything — Python, AI models, caches, temporary files — is installed inside this folder, not on C:.
  2. Double-click install.bat. The first run downloads about 10 GB and can take a long time.
  3. When asked about the Sucial male/female separation model, answer yes only if you accept its non-commercial licence. Without it, duet mode uses the aufr33 model.
  4. Wait for Done. Double-click start.bat to open SIGN2MV.

If installation stops (network drop, full disk), run install.bat again: finished steps are skipped. The log is in logs\install.log. On the Home page, Installation and the checks list show what is ready.

An NVIDIA graphics card is used automatically when present. Without one everything still works on the CPU, only slower (training on the CPU is very slow).

3. Start and shut down

  1. Double-click start.bat. A console window (the server) and a SIGN2MV window open. Keep the console window open while you work.
  2. To finish, press Shut down at the top right. If jobs are running or a workspace is busy (recording, converting, rendering), SIGN2MV lists them and asks before cancelling them.

The interface language follows Windows; change it with the language menu at the top. The Manual link opens this page.

4. Song library

  1. Open Library, type a title and press Add song.
  2. Import the music: paste a YouTube URL and press Import from YouTube (the video is downloaded too, up to 1080p), or choose an audio/video file and press Upload a file (up to 2 GB and 15 minutes). The original is kept unchanged.
  3. When the import finishes, the song shows its original player and three buttons: Sing this song, Make an MV, AI cover.

Stems and key

Under Stems and key you can prepare and listen to the separated tracks: choose the separator (Demucs, BS-RoFormer, Mel-RoFormer Kim), optionally a second pass on the instrumental and a lead/backing split, and a key. Separation is cached per song: any workspace that needs the same stems reuses them.

Saved stems (below the Prepare button) lists every separation and key copy this song keeps, as a tree: a lead/backing split sits under the separation it was made from. Each row shows its tracks, size, date and which cover versions or sing & record references use it. Delete removes that copy and everything made from it. Nothing breaks: whatever uses it separates again the next time it needs it (that takes a few minutes). Deleting waits while a job for the song is running.

Click a section title (Original, Stems and key, Saved stems, Storage) to fold or unfold it; the page remembers which ones you keep open. A folded section still shows a one-line summary. In Saved stems, ▸ N below shows or hides the copies made from that row.

Storage

The library list shows each song's size, and under it the library total and the free space on the drive. A song's Storage section splits its size into original, stems & key copies, finished AI covers, cover cache, sing & record, MV projects and other, with where to clean up each one:

  • Stems & key copies: delete them one by one in Saved stems.
  • AI covers and their cache: delete versions on the AI cover page; the cache that only those versions used is cleaned up with them.
  • Sing & record: delete takes and prepared references on the Sing & record page.
  • MV: delete projects on the MV page.

Rename changes the title. Delete removes the song and everything made from it (stems, recordings, covers, MVs) after a confirmation.

5. Sing & record

This is the former sing & record app, with the song chosen from the library. Pick the song at the top of the page (or press Sing this song in the library).

  1. Prepare the song: choose the key (−12 to +12, tempo unchanged) and press Prepare song reference. Wait until the status says ready. The default is full-song analysis with RMVPE pitch.
  2. Microphone: choose browser capture, or native capture if enabling the microphone lowers your backing audio; enable the microphone and check the input.
  3. Sing: choose what you hear (original vocals + backing, or backing/harmonies only) and what is recorded (singer only, mixed, or nothing), then Sing from the start. Stop, or let the song end; the take waits under Pending review. Save it for post-production when satisfied.
  4. Segments: enable segment mode, split the song while it plays, record each segment as many times as you like, choose a take per segment and assemble the full mix. Original-vocal regions can be added to the assembled mix.
  5. Post-production: levels, timing correction, smoothing, EQ, compression, reverb/delay, noise and breath reduction, pitch correction; compare A/B on the same phrase; render again (the original vocal is always kept).
  6. Make an MV from a finished recording: the recording is saved as an MV audio of the song and the MV workspace opens with it.

Masks in Exclude incorrect reference regions stop wrong reference notes (instrumental passages) from counting in the score.

6. AI cover

Open AI cover (or press AI cover on a song in the library) and pick the song at the top. The left side is the form; the right side holds the versions, voice models and training.

Make a cover

  1. Voice model: choose a model (and, if it has them, one of its kept training epochs). The last model you used is selected for new songs. If the list is empty, import or train a model first (chapter 7).
  2. Pitch: semitones for the voice (about −12 for female→male, +12 for male→female).
  3. Key (whole song): −8 to +8. The accompaniment and original backing vocals are shifted with Rubber Band (tempo unchanged) and the voice conversion gets the same shift. ▶ Preview this key plays the original in that key.
  4. Optional Conversion fine-tuning: consonant/breath protect (0.33), index rate (0.75), volume envelope (0 = follow the original singer's loudness), pitch detection (rmvpe), auto-tune, noise clean-up.
  5. Optional Separation and backing vocals: separator; vocals = convert the lead and keep the original backing vocals (default), convert all vocals, or split twice for songs with three or more voices; accompaniment single or second pass; whether the original backing vocals are mixed into the cover.
  6. Mix: converted vocal volume (95%) and accompaniment (85%).
  7. Press 🎤 Make cover (new version). The progress appears under the button and in the job queue.

Separation and conversions are cached. Running again with the same settings reuses them; changing only the mix is fast.

Versions

Every run creates a version (#1, #2, …) with its own MP3. On a version:

Load settingsputs that version's settings back into the form.
🎛️ Quick remixapplies the form's mix, vocal sections, volume sections, backing-vocal choice and duet volumes to this version and replaces its audio. Model, pitch, key and separation stay as they were.
Remix as new versionthe same, but keeps the old version and adds a new one.
🎬 Make MVopens the MV workspace with this version as the audio.
Download / Stemsdownload the MP3; listen to the original, separated tracks and the converted vocal used by this version.
Rename / Deletedeleting also deletes the version's audio; cached files no other version uses are cleaned up.

Every version also appears as MV audio of the song.

Vocal sections

Play a version or the original and use ⏱ to take the player time for From / to. For each section choose:

  • Original singer — this part uses the original vocal (level-matched), for example where someone else sings.
  • Mute — no vocal in this part.
  • Re-convert — this part is converted again with another model, pitch or settings (1 s of context on each side). Optionally the section is separated again with its own separator and lead split; its accompaniment is then replaced as well.
  • External clip — upload your own recording or another version. It replaces the converted vocal only, or the whole mix if the clip contains accompaniment. The clip's 0 s is placed at From + offset. A vocal clip can also be converted with a voice model.

Volume sections

Change the volume of the converted vocal, the accompaniment or the original backing vocals between two times (0–200%, 80 ms crossfades).

Male / female duet

Turn on Use male / female duet: the vocals are split into a male and a female line (aufr33 model, or Sucial if installed). Each side has its own model, pitch, volume and fine-tuning, or keeps the original singer. Changing only the two volumes later is a quick remix.

7. Voice models and training

Bring your existing models

  1. In AI cover → Voice models → Import from a folder, type D:\AI_COVER_MV\models (a folder of model folders) and press Import (copy). Every model folder with a .pth is copied with its .index, notes and kept epochs. The source is not changed.
  2. A single model folder or a single .pth file works too, and Upload a model accepts a .pth and an optional .index.

Model names use English letters, digits and _ . -. A folder whose name has other characters is skipped; import it on its own and type a name.

A model's Loss curve shows its training (mel and total loss per epoch; dashed lines mark the kept epoch versions). Kept epochs can be deleted one by one; the final version stays.

Train a voice model

  1. Type a model name, add recordings (upload files, or type folders/files on this computer, one per line). Clean solo singing or speech, about 10 minutes or more in total, works best.
  2. Defaults: 100 epochs, batch 8, a version saved every 10 epochs, 40 kHz.
  3. Press 🚀 Start training. The six steps run automatically: import, slicing, feature extraction (RMVPE + ContentVec), training, index, installing. The loss curve updates while it trains.
  4. When it finishes, the model is in the list with its last four epoch versions and loss curve. The training scratch folder is deleted.

⏹ Stop training stops it; the model is not installed. 🧹 Clean training leftovers removes scratch files of stopped trainings. Training uses the graphics card, so other GPU jobs wait until it ends.

8. MV

This is the former MV studio. A project belongs to a song; open it from the library (Make an MV), from a recording or from a cover version.

  1. Create a project: choose the song, a project name, the title and credits. The cover singer and studio names on the title cards come from Settings.
  2. Audio: the song's original, a sing & record take, a cover version, or an uploaded file.
  3. Photos: upload one photo per scene (batch upload fills the empty slots in order).
  4. Lyrics: paste the lyrics with [Scene 01] markers and press the Whisper alignment button. Whisper listens to the separated vocals (from the song's stem cache) and times every line; you can adjust lines, interludes and karaoke timing afterwards.
  5. Preview single frames, then render: a 1080p 60 fps MP4 with karaoke colouring. Rendering is a GPU job; the previous MV is kept until the new one is finished. Verification frames are checked automatically.

The MV never shows the word “AI”: credits use the cover singer name from Settings.

9. Job queue

Jobs lists every job with its progress. GPU jobs (separation, conversion, Whisper, rendering, training) run one at a time; the others wait with their position. General jobs (imports, key changes, quick remixes) run beside them. Cancel stops a job and its helper programs.

10. Settings

  • Library folder — where songs are stored (default library inside the SIGN2MV folder).
  • Cover singer name and Studio name — shown on the MV title cards.

11. Where things are stored

Everything a song uses is one of three kinds:

  • Original (source): kept as long as the song exists.
  • Caches: stems (stems, one copy per separation setting), key copies (keys, one copy per track and key), sing & record preparation, the cover cache. Every workspace shares them: whichever needs a copy first makes it, the others reuse it. Deleting a cache never breaks anything; it is made again when needed.
  • Your work: sing & record takes, AI cover versions, MV projects and videos. Deleting them is final. Takes and cover versions can be used as MV audio.

The cover's Pitch only changes the converted voice and is kept in the cover cache; the Key setting (in the library, sing & record and AI cover) uses the shared key copies.

Folder (inside SIGN2MV)Contents
library\songs\<song>\source (original), stems, keys, sing, renders (takes and cover versions used as MV audio), cover (cover versions and caches), mv (MV projects)
library\recordings\sing & record takes of all songs (the format sign_record uses)
models\AI models; models\voices = your RVC voice models
runtime\Python environments, Applio, ffmpeg, caches, temporary files, training scratch
config\, logs\settings, logs

To uninstall, shut SIGN2MV down and delete its folder (copy library and models\voices first if you want to keep them).

12. Troubleshooting

“Cannot reach the server”The console window was closed. Run start.bat again.
The page says to reload or restartPress F5, or Shut down and run start.bat again.
A model / component is missingRun install.bat again; the Home page lists the checks.
Voice conversion is not installedThe Applio part of the installation is incomplete: run install.bat again.
Key change failsffmpeg without Rubber Band: run install.bat again.
A job is slowCheck whether another GPU job (for example a training) is running in Jobs.
The voice index seems ignoredKeep the SIGN2MV folder path in English letters (for example D:\AI_SIGN2MV): the index library cannot open paths with Chinese characters.

Server details are in logs\server.log.
