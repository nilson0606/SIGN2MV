Choose a song. Record. Make your mix.

The detailed guide to the controls of the SIGN2MV Sing & Record page. Installation, importing songs and the other workspaces are in the SIGN2MV manual (Manual ↗ at the top of the SIGN2MV window). Everything is processed on your computer. The page’s Theme menu offers Console (the default: raised panels, recessed inputs, fader-style sliders), Tech blue and Warm; your choice is remembered.

Daily workflow

  1. Import the song in the SIGN2MV Library (YouTube or a local file) and press Sing this song, or choose it under Song (SIGN2MV library) at the top of this page and press Load song.
  2. The first time for a song (or a key), press Prepare song reference and wait until it is ready. Keys that already have a reference are listed in the song menu and load right away.
  3. Choose your input mode and microphone. Enable the microphone and test your voice with headphones.
  4. Choose guide vocals and recording levels, then record the whole song with Sing from the start and score, or individual segments in recording mode.
  5. Preview your take. For segment mixed previews, adjust vocal and backing levels while listening. Save for post-production when satisfied; use Rescore to score a take again at another delay without singing again.
  6. Compare effects with A/B, render a new result, then download audio or create a music video.

Prepare a song and choose its key

  1. Choose the song under Song (SIGN2MV library) and press Load song. New songs are imported in the SIGN2MV Library first (YouTube or a local file of up to 2 GB and 15 minutes); a version with a clear original vocal separates and scores better.
  2. Choose the key, from −12 to +12 semitones. Tempo stays the same. The default is the original key.
  3. Click Prepare song reference. Wait until the status says ready; 100% separation alone is not the end of preparation.

New songs default to full-song analysis, RMVPE pitch extraction, and retained preview stems. Advanced settings offer other models, short test ranges, and workflows. Changing model, mode, or key creates or loads the corresponding version. After Import again (replace source) in the SIGN2MV library, older references are marked “Source replaced: made again when loaded” and are made again from the new source when loaded. Different key versions keep separate recordings and segment drafts.

RMVPE and existing versions

Changing the pitch extraction setting alone does not load a new reference. Prepare the reference to apply it. Compatible retained stems can be reused without separating again. Existing songs remain available in the library.

Preview the separated stems

Preview vocals and instrumental to check separation. A lead / harmony version also offers separate lead and harmony previews. Choose this mode before preparation if “Backing only” playback should keep the original harmony vocals. Separation can make mistakes, so listen before committing to a long conversion.

If a song has only scoring data, use Rebuild preview stems. Stem preview pauses the video and does not score your voice.

Exclude incorrect reference regions from scoring

Load a reference, find an instrumental or misidentified passage, and enter its start and end in seconds or min:sec. Add and save the mask. Masked regions show Rest and do not count toward pitch, timing, or completeness; the video continues normally. Masks save locally, are shared across models for the same media and analysis range, and are fixed for the current singing round.

Record a whole song

Choose browser capture, or native capture if enabling the microphone lowers your backing audio. Native capture requires the local Python capture environment. Enable the microphone and check input first.

Under Guide vocals while singing (and Guide vocals while recording for segments), select original vocals + backing (the default), or backing / harmonies only. A version with separate lead and harmony vocals shows the default as Original vocals + harmonies + backing and adds Original vocals + backing (no harmonies), which plays only the lead and the accompaniment; a plain version does not offer it. The two non-default choices mute the video and play the saved separated stems instead, so they need retained stems. This is separate from what the recording saves. Choose singer-only, mixed recording, or no recording in the recording settings.

Click Sing from the start and score. Recording starts with playback and pauses during pauses or buffering. Pressing play on the player directly only scores; it does not begin a recording.

Stop, review, then approve

Stop input, finish and score, or let the song end. Whole-song takes are kept under Pending review. Preview or delete a take, then Save for post-production when satisfied. A pending take remains available without being promoted to a finished result; Download this take saves a copy. If the page is reloaded or closed, or capture breaks off, during a take, what was recorded is completed and moved to the library the next time the page opens, marked “Recovered after an interruption”, and kept under Pending review. Leaving the page while a take is being finished and saved asks first. A covered, minimised or background window keeps capturing while a take starts, runs or is being finished; an idle microphone is released after a minute in the background. Plugging other audio devices in or out (a Bluetooth headset, monitor speakers) no longer stops input; only unplugging the microphone in use does.

Sing from the start and score works at any time, also during a take or while a start is still in progress: the running take is ended first (kept as a pending take, not scored), then a new one starts from 0 s. If the microphone closes or a step gets stuck during the start, the status line names the step; press the button again. Finish and score pauses the video at once and waits at most 20 s for the RMVPE score; if the SIGN2MV server does not answer, the take is scored with the browser estimate instead.

Record and replace individual segments

  1. Load a song and enable recording mode. Click Split here during playback to create boundaries.
  2. On the timeline, the selected segment is bright and the others are dimmed; the segment being recorded blinks. Drag a boundary, enter an exact mm:ss.sss time, or step it earlier / later. Preview three seconds either side of a boundary. Segments must remain continuous and at least 0.1 seconds long.
  3. Select a segment. Choose pre-roll (default 5 seconds) and tail allowance (default 2 seconds). Loop practice does not record.
  4. Click Record this segment. It saves automatically after the tail, or use Stop / Save. Record as many takes as needed. Once a take is saved the microphone is closed; Record this segment opens it again. While a take is being saved (“Saving this part’s take…”) the next one cannot start.
  5. Choose a take for each segment. Preview the selected take with vocals + backing, enable manual levels, and adjust the 0–200% vocal and backing sliders while listening. Playback continues from the same position.
  6. Assemble the full mix. Mix content is vocals + backing / harmonies (the default) or vocals only; a lead / harmony version also offers Vocals + backing (no harmonies), which leaves the harmony track out of the assembly and of the saved result, so post-production does not add it back. The vocal correction starts at the Input latency (default 400 ms) and follows it; once changed here, that number is remembered and no longer follows. Takes are stored raw and the correction is applied only when previewing or assembling, so it can be set before or after recording. You can also adjust levels during this preview. Save for post-production or download WAV to render the latest levels. An already assembled mix can save the latest levels directly.

Original takes are kept, so level changes do not require another performance. Raw vocals-only preview plays the original input without balance or timing correction. If a region has no usable take, record it or explicitly allow silence. Changing take selections, boundaries, timing, or original-vocal regions requires a new assembly.

Draft boundaries and selections are stored in the SIGN2MV window’s browser data and saved automatically with every change; takes are saved in the library. The take list is read again whenever you come back to the sing page. Clearing this song’s segment recordings preserves boundaries, other songs, whole-song recordings, and saved production results.

Add original vocals to parts of a segment mix

Under original vocal regions, enter start and end in song time and choose Original + my recording or Original only. Regions cannot overlap. The current segment’s times can be filled in with one button.

These regions apply to the full mixed assembly. Outside them, your own vocals are used, with continuous backing. Original vocals use the current song key and do not receive microphone correction. Raw vocal preview ignores these regions. Assemble again after editing them.

Preview, compare, and produce

Select a saved recording. Preview plays its finished mix; Vocals only plays the separate singer track with saved production settings. Settings changed afterward require rendering before the finished player reflects them.

A/B comparison

A and B start with the recording’s saved settings and can be adjusted separately. B is selected initially. Select the same phrase, up to 30 seconds, and switch A/B previews at the same playback position. Optional loudness matching reduces the louder version for comparison only. Trim and fades are shared and are not applied in clip comparison. Switching recordings or reloading clears temporary A/B edits.

Levels, timing, and effects

100% preserves the recorded balance; 0% mutes. Positive timing correction moves late vocals earlier; negative values move early vocals later. Enter a total correction, not an additional increment. Backing stays fixed. New recordings are corrected by the input latency (default 400 ms).

Available vocal processing includes smoothing, EQ, compression, reverb, delay, region levels and effects, noise reduction, de-essing, breath reduction, and pitch correction. Try short clips and change one control at a time. Mood presets change tone; space presets change reverb and delay. Cleanup and pitch correction have independent controls.

Region times use the full recording after timing correction and before trimming, not song time. Regions cannot overlap. Recheck them after timing changes. Reverb tails can extend beyond regions.

Save your preferred version

Select A or B, set trim and fades, then Render again. This saves a new editable result while preserving the original. Processing always starts from the original vocals, so effects and trimming do not accumulate. Leave the trim end blank to keep the full reverb tail. Download WAV or convert to MP3 afterward.

Input latency

One setting, Input latency (default +400 ms), is used both for live scoring and for the vocal correction of whole-song recordings: vocals are lined up 0.40 seconds earlier, once, never added twice. It is remembered after you change it. After a take, a new Scoring delay (or Rescore) is used for the next takes of the same song; another song or a reload goes back to this setting. Take to rescore picks what is rescored: the take just sung, or a saved whole-song recording of this song and key (pending or saved to post-production, newest first; choosing one fills in the delay it was recorded with), so after a reload or a restart you can rescore an earlier take at another delay or difficulty without singing again. The first rescore of a saved recording runs RMVPE on its clean vocal for a few seconds and keeps the result with that recording; after that the score is immediate. It uses the difficulty chosen above Sing from the start and score. Rescoring is off while a take runs. Under AI cover the menu also lists the AI cover versions of this song and key (the cover key must match the loaded reference). Their converted vocal is scored; it is on the song’s own timeline, so the scoring delay starts at 0 and changing it never touches the input latency. The version’s own pitch (e.g. −12 for another voice) is taken out for scoring, so the score says how well the conversion kept the melody. The first rescore after opening the page runs RMVPE for a few seconds; nothing is written into the cover’s files. Versions with male and female converted separately only get the main pitch taken out, so their score may be off. An original vocal test does not change it. A segment mix can be rescored too; its octave matching is saved with it (older segment mixes saved without it use the current Octave matching setting). Original vocals remain available for production, and each take can be adjusted in post-production.

The optional Do Re Mi Fa Sol test measures singing response or speaker-to-microphone loopback. Review the suggested value before applying it. It does not save audio. Bluetooth, USB, wired, and built-in devices can differ; changing microphones keeps your input latency, so adjust it yourself if a device needs a different value.

Standard difficulty is the default; choose it right above Sing from the start and score. Standard gives full pitch marks within 30 cents and none from 105 cents (a semitone plus a little), and allows notes 0.1 s early or late; relaxed widens this to 50 cents, 150 cents and 0.2 s; strict narrows it to 20 cents, 55 cents and no timing slack. Standard and strict keep 5 cents for measurement error: the original vocal of 童話, run through 🧪 Score the original vocal in the original key, reaches 100 on pitch only from there. Rhythm is timed on each note start of the reference: the moment the voice arrives on that note (closer to it than to the note before, within 150 cents; after silence, where the voice starts) is found the same way in the reference and in your take, and the two are compared, so a few cents off never turns a whole note start into zero; pitch is marked by the pitch score. Choose relaxed or strict, octave tolerance, and performed-only or whole-song scoring before a round. Pauses are unscored, but missed and skipped parts reduce the score. Rescoring uses the original recording data and your selected reference and difficulty; audio production does not rewrite the performance score.

🧪 Score the original vocal (next to Finish and score) uses the separated original vocal instead of the microphone and runs exactly the same scoring and recording as your own take. It shows what the song’s reference and scoring give the original singer, so a low score can be traced to the reference or to the singing. The original vocal skips the “hear it, then sing” delay a singer has, so Delay the original vocal (200 ms by default, remembered) brings it in that much later. The take is labelled “original vocal test” and does not change the input latency.

Create a music video

Select a saved recording and click Create music video. The recording, with its saved levels, effects and trim, is saved as MV audio of the song and the SIGN2MV MV page opens (Quick MV or Full MV). The videos are played, downloaded and deleted on the MV page. Render production changes and select the new result before you create the video.

Saved data and cleanup

The SIGN2MV library stores song references, optional stems, recordings and finished results; sing & record takes are in library\recordings, one folder per take. A take still being saved stays in the browser data until it is written; if that fails, the browser copy is kept. Preferences, score history and segment drafts are kept in the SIGN2MV window’s browser data.

Deletion dialogs describe their scope. Pending takes, segment takes, production recordings, backing tracks, temporary conversions and music videos have separate cleanup controls. Deletions are permanent. Active conversion files and the current preview are protected by temporary-file cleanup.

Troubleshooting

  • The page cannot reach the server: the SIGN2MV console window was closed. Run start.bat again.
  • Microphone denied: allow it when the SIGN2MV window asks, and check the microphone privacy setting in Windows Settings and the selected input device; with native capture, allow Python to use the microphone. Then enable it again.
  • Missing stems: load the correct song version and rebuild preview stems. Backing-only guidance and mixed production need those files.
  • Harmonies scored as the melody (lead / harmony versions): when the reference is made, a run of melody where the harmony track is clearly louder than the lead (by 6 dB or more, median over the run) or the lead is nearly silent (below −45 dBFS) becomes a rest and is not scored. Lead / harmony references made before 2026-11-09 are made again once the next time they are loaded (pitch analysis only, about a minute). If a sung lead line turns into a rest, report the song and the time.
  • Levels seem unchanged: raw vocal preview ignores mix balance. For live segment mixing, select vocals + backing and enable manual levels. For saved production, render again after editing.
  • Scores stay low: check that octave differences are allowed (when your range differs from the original), then try a few scoring delays with Rescore; 🧪 Score the original vocal shows whether the reference is sound.
  • Old interface: press F5 after an update; if the page says the server is older, use Shut down and run start.bat again.
  • Interrupted recording: inspect the recording status and download any retained unsaved audio before closing the page. Input logs are in logs\native-capture.jsonl.

Data and usage

Microphone analysis, separation, recordings and production run on this computer; nothing is uploaded. Importing from YouTube needs the network, and the installer downloads the programs and models once.
